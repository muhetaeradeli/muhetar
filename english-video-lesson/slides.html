●Healthy Eating HabitsStep 1 · Reading (Diffit)

Eat Smart, Feel Great

Many teenagers skip breakfast because they are in a hurry. However, breakfast gives your brain energy for morning lessons. A healthy breakfast can be simple: an egg, some bread and a piece of fruit.
Another problem is sugary drinks. One can of cola can contain almost nine teaspoons of sugar. Water is the best choice — and it is free!
Fast food is tasty, but it often has too much salt and fat. You don't have to stop eating it — just eat it less often. Try to fill half of your plate with vegetables and fruit.
Remember: healthy eating is about balance, not about being perfect. Small changes can become good habits.
