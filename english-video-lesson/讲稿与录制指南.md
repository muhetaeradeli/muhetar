# Healthy Eating Habits — 视频课讲稿与录制指南

- 主题：Healthy Eating Habits　对象：14–16 岁，B1　工具：Diffit / Twee / MagicSchool AI
- 成片结构：`lesson_video_silent.mp4`（无声课件，3:35）+ 你自己录的 3 段工具操作录屏（每段约 20 秒）+ 你的配音 ≈ **4:35**
- 讲稿语速约每分钟 130 词。哪页讲不完，就在 CapCut 里把那一页拉长。

---

## 一、先录 3 段工具录屏（作业要求 "show the actual use"，必须是你自己的账号和操作）

| # | 工具 | 操作步骤（照着做） | 插入位置 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Diffit** (web.diffit.me) | 首页 → 输入主题 `Healthy eating habits for teenagers: breakfast, sugary drinks, fast food, balance` → 阅读等级选 **Grade 7**（约等于 B1）→ Generate → 往下滚动，展示生成的 reading、vocabulary 和 questions | 视频 **1:08**（Diffit 标题卡之后） |
| B | **Twee** (twee.com) | 登录 → 选 **Vocabulary → Match words with definitions**，粘贴课文，生成 → 再选 **Fill in the gaps** 生成 → 再选 **Discussion questions**，Topic 填 `healthy eating`，Level 选 **B1** | 视频 **1:34**（Twee 标题卡之后） |
| C | **MagicSchool** (magicschool.ai) | 搜索 **Multiple Choice Quiz** → Grade level 选 **9th grade**，题数 `5`，粘贴课文 → Generate → 回到工具列表搜 **Rubric Generator**，填 `Speaking task: give advice using should/shouldn't about healthy eating, B1` → Generate | 视频 **2:30**（MagicSchool 标题卡之后） |

**重要**：课件里的课文、练习和测验是我写的初稿。录屏时工具生成的内容会跟课件不一样，这很正常。处理方法：**把 AI 生成的结果和课件对照，自己改一两处，录屏时讲出你改了什么**（例如 "AI made the text too long, so I shortened it"）。这样就满足了 "check, edit and adapt" 的要求。最好把你最终确认的版本更新到课件里，我可以帮你重新生成视频。

## 二、配音讲稿（按页，时间对应无声视频）

**[0:00 · 标题页 10s]**
Hello everyone, and welcome to today's English lesson! Today's topic is "Healthy Eating Habits."

**[0:10 · About this lesson 12s]**
This lesson is for teenagers aged fourteen to sixteen at B1, intermediate level. We will practise reading, vocabulary and speaking.

**[0:22 · Objectives 18s]**
By the end of the lesson, you will be able to: understand and use eight new words about food and health; read a short text and find the main ideas; give advice with "should" and "shouldn't"; and talk about your own eating habits.

**[0:40 · Tools 12s]**
Today I'm using three digital tools: Diffit for our reading text, Twee for vocabulary and speaking tasks, and MagicSchool AI to check your learning.

**[0:52 · Warm-up 12s]**
Let's start with a quick question. What did you eat for breakfast today? … (pause) Is it healthy? Why, or why not? Think for a few seconds and type your answer in the chat.

**[1:04 · Diffit 标题卡 4s]** Step one: reading, with Diffit.

> ▶ **插入录屏 A（Diffit）**，边操作边说：
> I type my topic into Diffit and choose the reading level for my students. In a few seconds, Diffit creates a text, key vocabulary and questions. I checked the text and made it shorter and simpler for my class.

**[1:08 · Reading 22s]**
Here is our text, "Eat Smart, Feel Great." Please read it silently. Look at the highlighted words — they are our new vocabulary. … (pause) So, what is the main idea? Yes — healthy eating is about balance, not about being perfect.

**[1:30 · Twee 标题卡 4s]** Step two: vocabulary and speaking, with Twee.

> ▶ **插入录屏 B（Twee）**：
> Now I'm using Twee. I paste my text, and Twee makes a matching exercise, a gap-fill and discussion questions. I chose the five most useful words for my students.

**[1:34 · Matching 18s]**
Match the words with their meanings. Number one, "skip" … that's "d" — to not do or not have something. Now try the others on your own. … Good! 2-a, 3-e, 4-c, 5-b.

**[1:52 · Gap fill 12s]**
Now fill in the gaps with the words in the box. You have ten seconds. … (pause)

**[2:04 · Answers 6s]**
Let's check: skip, choice, salt, habit, balance. Well done!

**[2:10 · Speaking 16s]**
Time to speak! Read about Aziz, Lola and Tom, and give them advice with "should" or "shouldn't." For example: "Aziz should drink water. He shouldn't drink so much cola." Now you — what about Lola?

**[2:26 · MagicSchool 标题卡 4s]** Step three: assessment, with MagicSchool AI.

> ▶ **插入录屏 C（MagicSchool）**：
> To check your understanding, I use MagicSchool. The Multiple Choice Quiz tool creates questions from my text, and the Rubric Generator helps me assess speaking fairly. I edited the answer options to match my lesson.

**[2:30 · Quiz 18s]**
Here is a quick quiz with five questions. Choose a, b or c. … The answers are: a, b, c, b, c. How many did you get right?

**[2:48 · Rubric 12s]**
This is how I assess your speaking: vocabulary, grammar with "should" and "shouldn't," and fluency. Each one gets from one to three points.

**[3:00 · Exit ticket 8s]**
Before we finish: what is one healthy habit you will start this week? Write two or three sentences in the chat.

**[3:08 · Conclusion 22s]**
To sum up: Diffit gives every student a text at the right level. Twee makes quick, interactive practice for vocabulary and speaking. And MagicSchool helps me make quizzes and assess fairly. AI saves a lot of time — but the teacher must always check and edit the materials.

**[3:30 · Thank you 5s]**
Your homework: write a healthy menu for one day. Thank you, and see you next time!

## 三、CapCut 剪辑步骤

1. 导入 `lesson_video_silent.mp4` 和 3 段录屏
2. 在 1:08、1:34、2:30 三个位置分割（Split），按顺序插入录屏 A、B、C
3. 用 Record（录音）功能，看着画面逐页配音；某页讲不完就把该页拉长
4. 可选：开 Auto captions 生成字幕；在右下角加你的摄像头小窗，会更像网课
5. 导出 1080p，确认总时长 4–5 分钟
